# Designer changes, 16 November 2026, eleventh round: baby designs

Twenty-one new designs built from Shaun's clipart. Apply after rounds c to j: this file's script uses helpers defined in those scripts.

## What's new

**Thirteen picture designs** where the customer's words are the headline. They lead the baby occasions; Stork Delivery (green bundle) is the new default.
`bb-stork-green`, `bb-stork-pink`, `bb-stork-blue` Stork Delivery; `bb-frame-pink`, `bb-frame-blue` Bow Frame (words inside); `bb-horse-pink`, `bb-horse-blue` Rocking Horse; `bb-line-pink`, `bb-line-blue` Baby Clothesline; `bb-onesie-pink`, `bb-onesie-blue` Onesie; `bb-bottles` Baby Bottles; `bb-pacifiers` Pacifiers.

All thirteen show for Baby shower, New baby, Pregnancy announcement and Gender reveal. Baptism and Adoption are unchanged.

**Eight lettering designs** (headline is artwork):
- New baby, Gender reveal, Baby shower: `bb-block-girl`, `bb-block-boy` (fixed colors), `bb-script-girl`, `bb-script-boy` (print in the chosen color).
- Gender reveal only: `bb-heorshe` He or She?, `bb-whatwill` What Will It Be?, `bb-teampink` Team Pink, `bb-teamblue` Team Blue.

Not used: the "Congratulations" script on the two cards (too fine to print at label size), the other seven frame shapes on each bow-frame sheet, and the loose words "Girl" and "Boy".
