# Designer changes, 16 November 2026, tenth round: St. Patrick's Day designs

Twelve new designs for the St. Patrick's Day occasion, built from Shaun's clipart. Apply after rounds c to i: this file's script uses helpers defined in those scripts.

## What's new

**Five picture designs** where the customer's words are the headline. They lead the St. Patrick's Day list; Pot of Gold is the new default.
`sp-pot` Pot of Gold, `sp-shamrock` Watercolor Shamrock, `sp-lucky` Lucky Charms (hat, shamrock, mug, horseshoe, flag), `sp-hat` Leprechaun Hat, `sp-bunting` Irish Bunting.

**Seven lettering designs** (headline is artwork): `sp-brush` Saint Patrick's Brush (prints in the chosen color), `sp-c1` to `sp-c4` (four "St. Patrick's Day" calligraphy styles, fixed green and orange), `sp-belucky` Be Lucky, `sp-luckyword` Lucky.

Not used: the pipe-and-shamrock picture, the calligraphy style with feathers, and the plain ribbon banner, hearts, balloons and string lights from the watercolor sheet.

## What to add to the theme

Nothing existing changes. Add:

1. **One new script file:** `js/05i-designs-stpatrick.js`, loaded straight after `js/05h-designs-valentine.js` and before `js/06-app.js`.
