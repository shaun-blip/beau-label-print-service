# Designer changes, 16 November 2026, eighth round: graduation designs

Twenty new designs built from Shaun's clipart. Apply after rounds c to g: this file's script uses helpers defined in those scripts.

## What's new

**Eight picture designs** where the customer's words are the headline. They lead the Graduation list; Cap & Diploma is the new default.
`gr-capdiploma` Cap & Diploma, `gr-scroll` Diploma Scroll (the words sit inside the scroll), `gr-caps` Caps Off, `gr-capfloral` Floral Cap, `gr-party` Grad Party, `gr-toast` Graduation Toast, `gr-cake` Graduation Cake, `gr-disco` Disco Ball.

**Twelve lettering designs** (headline is artwork, fixed colors):
- Graduation only: `gr-congrats` Congrats Cap, `gr-gradparty1` to `gr-gradparty4`.
- Graduation plus New job, Promotion, Retirement, New home, You did it, Engagement: `gr-rainbow` Rainbow Congrats, `gr-congrats1` to `gr-congrats3`.
- Those occasions plus every birthday, Anniversary and New Year's: `gr-cheers1` to `gr-cheers3`.

Not used: "Class of 2024" and "Class of 2025" lettering (the years are past), the three "Congratulation" badges (singular in the artwork), and the two sets of invitation cards (they carry sample names, dates and addresses).
