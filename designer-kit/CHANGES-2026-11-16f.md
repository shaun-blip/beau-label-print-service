# Designer changes, 16 November 2026, sixth round: Hanukkah designs

Twelve new designs for the Hanukkah occasion, built from Shaun's clipart. Apply after rounds c, d and e: this file's script uses helpers defined in those scripts.

## What's new

**Nine picture designs** where the customer's words are the headline. They lead the Hanukkah list; Watercolor Menorah is the new default.
`hk-wmenorah` Watercolor Menorah, `hk-wrow` Hanukkah Watercolors, `hk-goldmenorah` Golden Menorah (royal blue background, white words), `hk-bluemenorah` Blue Menorah, `hk-dove` Peace Dove (lilac background), `hk-dreidel` Dreidel & Bow (pink background), `hk-smenorah` Menorah Sketch and `hk-srow` Hanukkah Sketches (line drawings that print in the chosen color), `hk-stickers` Hanukkah Stickers (blue background).

**Three lettering designs** (headline is artwork): `hk-title` Happy Hanukkah Script (with the pale garden from the card down both sides), `hk-brush` Hanukkah Brush (prints in the chosen color), `hk-ktitle` Happy Hannukah Sticker.

Note for Shaun: the sticker sheet spells it "Hannukah" (two Ns, one K). The rest of the designer says "Hanukkah". `hk-ktitle` keeps the artwork's spelling and is named to match; remove that one entry from `HK_LETTERING` if he doesn't want it.

Not used: the hamsa and the Torah scroll from the watercolor sheet, the stickers that carry extra words ("Cool!", "Let's go", "It's time", "Amazing", "Joy"), and the Hebrew letters from the sketch sheet.
