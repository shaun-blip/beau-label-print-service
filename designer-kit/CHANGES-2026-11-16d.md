# Designer changes, 16 November 2026, fourth round: birthday and celebration designs

Thirty-three new designs built from Shaun's clipart. Apply after the Thanksgiving round (CHANGES-2026-11-16c.md); this file's script depends on a helper defined in the Thanksgiving script.

## What's new

**Three picture designs** where the customer's words are the headline. They lead the birthday list, and Confetti Bunting is now the birthday default:
`bd-bunting` Confetti Bunting, `bd-candles` Birthday Candles, `bd-cupcake` Cupcake.

**Thirty designs where the lettering is the artwork** (ready-made sayings are locked; the name-or-note line prints under it). They sit after the existing birthday designs.

- Birthday (all birthday occasions, Birthday through Any age): `bd-gold` Gold Glitter Birthday (navy background), `bd-pink` Pink Shimmer Birthday (plum background), `bd-l1` to `bd-l6` (black and gold scripts), `bd-b1` to `bd-b10` (colour badges), `bd-balloon`, `bd-makewish`, `bd-wish` (prints in the chosen ink colour).
- Birthday and congratulations occasions: `bd-w1` Yay!, `bd-w2` Woo Hoo, `bd-w8` Hooray!, `bd-w5` Cheers! (also Anniversary, Work anniversary, Girls' trip), `bd-w9` Enjoy! (birthday plus Hostess, Just because, Neighbor, Dinner party, Thank you).
