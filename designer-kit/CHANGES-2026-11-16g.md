# Designer changes, 16 November 2026, seventh round: Fourth of July designs

Thirteen new designs for the Fourth of July occasion, built from Shaun's clipart. Apply after rounds c to f: this file's script uses helpers defined in those scripts.

## What's new

**Nine picture designs** where the customer's words are the headline. They lead the Fourth of July list; Star Wreath is the new default.
`j4-starwreath` Star Wreath, `j4-bunting` Bunting Circle, `j4-flag` Old Glory, `j4-fireworks` Fireworks, `j4-liberty` Lady Liberty, `j4-unclesam` Uncle Sam Hat, `j4-balloons` Flag Balloons, `j4-rockets` Rockets, `j4-party` Americana Party.
All sit on a white label: the watercolors were painted on white paper and their white stripes are see-through, so the color step's background choice is fixed to white for these.

**Four lettering designs** (headline is artwork, fixed colors): `j4-burst` Happy 4th Burst, `j4-script` Fourth of July Script, `j4-happy4th` Happy 4th Star Wreath, `j4-4july` 4th of July Bunting.

Not used: the two awareness ribbons, the gift tags, the striped banner, and the party hat and pennants that run off the bottom of the sheet.
