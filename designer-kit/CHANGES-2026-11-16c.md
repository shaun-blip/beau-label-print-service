On the five headline-artwork designs the ready-made sayings are locked (same behaviour as the Christmas lettering designs); the name-or-note line prints under the artwork.

## What to add to the theme

Nothing existing changes. Add:

1. **One new script file:** `js/05b-designs-thanksgiving.js`, loaded straight after `js/05-designs-holiday-photo.js` and before `js/06-app.js`.
2. **32 new artwork files** in `assets/`, all named `xm-tg_*.webp`. Upload them as theme assets like the others.
3. **32 new `<image>` entries** in the hidden artwork `<svg><defs>` block of `designer.html`. They are the ones whose id starts `xm-tg_`; copy them from this kit's `designer.html` and point each `href` at the uploaded asset the same way the existing `xm-` images are pointed.

## Print service: redeploy needed this time

The print service draws from its own copy of the designer. Until it is redeployed with the new one-file designer, an order for a Thanksgiving design cannot be printed. From the kit folder run:

    python3 tools/build-single-file.py ../label-print-service/public/designer.html

then commit and push the print service so Render redeploys. Do this before the designs go live on the theme.
